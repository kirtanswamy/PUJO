# Illustrator brief: PUJO (Durga Pujo web experience)

*Send this document to the artist. Fill the [bracketed] items first.*

## 1. The project
PUJO is an interactive, ambient website that recreates one Bengali Durga Pujo night as a walk through a 90s to early-2000s Kolkata childhood. The visitor starts in a dark room at 4 AM with a radio playing Mahalaya, then walks from pandal to pandal across Shashthi, Saptami, Ashtami, Navami and Dashami. Think of the warmth and cleanliness of saloon.wtf and schoolkebaad.fun, but rooted in Bengali visual culture.

It is built to return **every year**, with a new pandal and art pack each Pujo. This commission is the first edition's art and the visual foundation for later ones.

## 2. The look
- **Flat vector illustration** in the tradition of **Kalighat pat** (bold outlines, flat colour, expressive faces) and **Kumartuli** clay idols, with **daaker saaj** (silver-foil ornament) as shimmering detail.
- Layered with the feel of **cheap 90s offset printing**: Pujo-sankhya covers, hand-painted shop signboards and banner lettering. We add grain and halftone in code, so please keep the SVG itself clean and flat.
- **Warm, slightly retro, atmospheric.** Not glossy, not 3D, not stock.
- Please send 5 to 10 references you would draw from before starting.

## 3. Non-negotiables
1. **No generative AI at any stage** (no AI image tools, no AI upscaling or fill). Hand-made work only.
2. **Reverent depiction of the deity.** Iconography must be accurate to a traditional Bengali protima (ten arms and their attributes, the lion, Mahishasura, Lakshmi, Saraswati, Kartik, Ganesh as in the family-group form, the chalchitra arch). A Bengali reviewer on our side will sign off at sketch stage.
3. **Expression:** calm, watchful, alive. Nothing menacing or distorted.

## 4. Technical spec (the layer-ID contract)
Deliver **SVG**. Each scene is one file, built from separate groups with these exact IDs so our code can animate them without edits:

`#sky`, `#structure`, `#chalchitra`, `#protima`, `#eye-l`, `#eye-r`, `#lights`, `#lamp-pradip`, `#dhunuchi-1` to `#dhunuchi-n`, `#foreground`, `#crowd`, `#alpona`

Rules:
- Flat fills only. No gradients, filters, masks or blend modes.
- No embedded raster images. Convert all text to outlines.
- `#eye-l` and `#eye-r` are separate shapes on top of the face, not baked into it.
- `#lamp-pradip` (the five-tiered brass lamp) and each dhunuchi pot are separate groups, centred so we can rotate or move them.
- Keep each scene file reasonably light (aim for under 300 KB).
- Scene composition is 4:5 portrait for the main subject, with sky and ground layers extending wider so they can bleed to the edges of a 16:9 screen.
- Also deliver the source file (Illustrator or Figma).

## 5. Deliverables, in priority order
**Round 1 (first):**
1. Protima with chalchitra, in a Thakurdalan-style setting (the hero image)
2. Ghor: a 90s Bengali room at 4 AM. Valve radio on a shelf or windowsill, a window showing pre-dawn sky and kaash flowers, a doorway
3. Style frame of the street between pandals (one wide image, to confirm the look)

**Round 2:**
4. Pandal exteriors: community (Baroaari) pandal and a themed pandal
5. Foreground silhouettes: dhunuchi dancers (separate pots), crowd heads, dhaaki with dhaak
6. Props as separate illustrations: puja thali (with flowers, lamp, conch, bell, dhunuchi), cassette deck, CRT TV, Pujo-sankhya magazine, bhog plate, loudspeaker on a bamboo pole

**Round 3:**
7. Saptami river dawn (Kola Bou), Sindoor Khela, Bishorjon river scene
8. Alpona patterns, string lights, marigold, lotus, shiuli and kaash flowers

## 6. Process
1. Rough sketches for approval (we give one round of consolidated feedback)
2. Flat colour version
3. Final SVG with correct IDs
We check each stage against the spec; please plan for one revision round per stage.
