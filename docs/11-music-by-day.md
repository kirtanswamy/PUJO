# Music by day (first pass)

Plays through official embeds on the TV or radio prop (Phase 6), never as self-hosted files. Send official YouTube links with embedding enabled; see docs/06-licensing.md.
These are suggestions from the user's list, placed by mood. Rearrange freely.

| Day | Scene | Songs |
|---|---|---|
| Mahalaya | The room, 4 AM | Shehnai Bhimpalasi, Ya Chandi, Namo Debyoi, Mahishasuramardini, Aha ki ananda (the Mahalaya set) |
| Shashthi | Courtyard at dusk | Agomonir Gaan, Durge Durge Durgatinashini |
| Saptami | River at dawn | Dugga Elo, Amar Saptamir Bikel |
| Ashtami | Courtyard, morning to midnight | Ashtamite Tomar Paray, Dhak Baja Kashor Baja |
| Navami | Courtyard, night | Dhaker Tale Komor Dole, Ebar Jeno Onno Rokom Pujo |
| Dashami | Sindoor Khela, then the river at dusk | **Gap: needs one or two Bijoya / Bishorjon songs** |

Notes
- "Amar Saptamir Bikel" is a Saptami afternoon song; it sits oddly with a dawn scene. Consider moving it or making the Saptami scene a late afternoon.
- Owned sound layers (dhaak, crowd, bells, river) sit under the songs: see docs/09-audio-manifest.md.
- The original Mahalaya broadcast is the main licensing question. Check the rights holder before launch.
