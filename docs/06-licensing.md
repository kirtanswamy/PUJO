# Audio sources and licensing

Not legal advice. Every track in an edition is one of three kinds:

| Kind | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `owned` | Recorded by us, commissioned, or CC0/CC-BY with credit | Self-hosted. Log in `public/audio/CREDITS.md` |
| `youtube` | Official upload played through the YouTube IFrame API | Player must stay visible (embed docs set a minimum 200x200 viewport). Re-check YouTube developer policy on hiding the player or separating audio from video before heavy styling. Only videos with embedding enabled |
| `spotify` | Official Spotify embed | Full playback generally only for logged-in Spotify users, previews otherwise. Verify before depending on it |

## Rules
- Do not commit or self-host recordings we do not own or have licensed.
- For the famous Mahalaya recording: ask the rights holder about a license; keep the answer in this file.
- Commission an original recitation of the Chandi texts and re-record Tagore compositions as owned tracks.
- Prefer our own recorded pandal sound over internet sources.
