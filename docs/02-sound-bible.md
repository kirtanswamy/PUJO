# Sound bible

## Layers
1. Room tone / crowd
2. Dhaak (close and far versions per pandal, for distance mixing)
3. Kanshor, ghonta, shankh, ulu dhwani, dhunuchi crackle
4. Loudspeaker crackle and announcements
5. Music (via the TV / radio / cassette; see licensing)

## Behaviour
- Audio starts only after the entry-gate tap.
- Each pandal is a Web Audio source; gain and filtering follow walking distance.
- Mixer: separate sliders for crowd, dhaak, bells, music. Master mute always visible.
- Radio static between tracks; CRT static burst between channels.

## Recording checklist (a real pandal)
- 5+ min of room tone and crowd
- Dhaak close and 30–50 m away
- Aarti bells, shankh, ulu dhwani, dhunuchi crackle, loudspeaker
- WAV, 48 kHz / 24-bit; steady phone or recorder, out of wind
- Ask the dhaaki or committee for a quick OK to use the recording; log date, place, person
- Log every clip in `public/audio/CREDITS.md`

## Long-term
Commission a dhaaki and small band each year. Commission a Mahalaya-style recitation of the Chandi texts as an original performance.
